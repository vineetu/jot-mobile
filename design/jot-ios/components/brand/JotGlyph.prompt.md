Jot's stroke-based inline-SVG glyphs (mic, keyboard, check, sparkle, chevrons, close, arrow-up, doc, copy, trash, pause, globe, search, keyboard-mini, watch) — production iOS uses SF Symbols; these are the visual equivalents.

```jsx
<JotGlyph name="mic" size={56} color="#fff" />
<JotGlyph name="sparkle" size={34} color="var(--jot-blue-2)" />
```

The 4-point `sparkle` is the Ask Jot / AI motif. Never substitute emoji.
