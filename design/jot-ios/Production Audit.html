Discrepancies found — design system updated

FIXED

Coral is the shipped AI accent, not just two tiles. Cleanup tile, the ✦ sparkle on AI-rewritten recents, "+ New prompt" button text — all coral. Documented as --jot-ai-accent: current shipped AI identity, hated, never to be expanded.

FIXED

Serif has two modes. In-app page titles ("Settings.", "Help") are upright Fraunces SemiBold with a period — italic is reserved for onboarding titles, subtitles, and anything spoken (transcripts, questions, streaming text). The guide previously said italic everywhere.

FIXED

Transcript detail body is SF, not serif. Plain SF body on a big white card; the serif-italic transcript treatment belongs to live/streaming states (keyboard preview, recording, Ask hero) only.

FIXED

Transport order is pause · Stop · trash (Stop pill centered, trash right with red glyph on white circle) — the swipe-cue handoff had trash · pause · Stop. Recording reassurance copy also differs (see new strings).

FIXED

Light background is flatter and cooler than the prototype gradient — blue-grey wash, no visible warm corner. Noted that --jot-page-* (production) wins for in-app surfaces.

FIXED

New UI vocabulary captured: green caps badges (READY, ALWAYS), grey EXPERIMENTAL pill, event chips (PASTE/PUBLISH green, VOCAB blue), segmented Original/Rewrite control, "Articulate" ✦ CTA, keyboard RECENT panel ("Opens in Jot" / "Pastes here").
