Copy & guardrails

Exact copy (locked)

  • ›State labels: Recording Working on it… Mic on standby Saved & copied
  • ›Standby disclosure: “Tap Jot down to capture again, or End to release the mic.”
  • ›Buttons: Stop End Jot down

Guardrails

  • ✕No live transcript text in any pill — privacy & platform.
  • ✕No live amplitude / waveform meter — infeasible in a Live Activity.
  • ✓Stale must read as clearly inactive — no still-counting, live-looking pill.
  • ✓Banners carry buttons? No — lock-screen banner is dot · Jot · state · timer only.
