The locked Jot brand mark — lowercase stroked "j" with a 3-bar voice-waveform tittle; use white on dark/blue, `#1A8CFF` on light.

```jsx
<JotMark size={64} color="#fff" />
```

The 3 waveform bars must stay visually separate (never fused). For the full-color gradient or wordmark versions, use the SVGs in `assets/logo/` instead.
